# 3-6. The BCT/OSUT/AIT “Golden Rules”

Source: PDF page 47 (printed page 41). The section ends at the horizontal rule before Chapter 4.

## Complete Green Book section

### #1

**DO NOT** bully, haze, assault or harass a fellow Soldier.

**DO** help and assist your teammate.

### #2

**DO NOT** use vulgar language, rude gestures, or discriminate against others.

**DO** treat everyone with dignity and respect.

### #3

**DO NOT** kiss, attempt to kiss, or touch a fellow Soldier.

**DO** respect your teammate's personal space.

### #4

**DO NOT** steal or take something that does not belong to you.

**DO** build trust with teammates through your ethical and disciplined actions.

### #5

**DO NOT** go anywhere without your battle buddy.

**DO** report violations of policies and regulations to your platoon and company leadership.

## Proposed input and grading plan

- Present five numbered cards, one for each source rule.
- Each card contains a `DO NOT` input and its paired `DO` input. The labels can be fixed UI prefixes; the learner supplies the clause that follows each label.
- Treat each pair as one scored item, like the app's composite rank/pay-grade cards. Show partial feedback when only one half is correct.
- Ignore case, punctuation, checkmark symbols, line breaks, and whitespace. Require every word of each clause in order and do not accept paraphrases.
- Preserve the pairing: a correct positive action attached to the wrong prohibition is not a fully correct rule.
- Easy mode gives first-letter hints within each clause. Hard mode gives only the rule number and the `DO NOT`/`DO` structural labels.
- Show a two-line canonical correction for an incomplete pair.
