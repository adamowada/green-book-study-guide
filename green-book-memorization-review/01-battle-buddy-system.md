# 3-3. The Battle Buddy System

Source: PDF pages 43-45 (printed pages 37-39). The red star is beside the section heading; the section ends immediately before `3-4. Suicide prevention`.

## Complete Green Book section

Soldiers rely on one another to stay motivated and reach peak performance. Although required in Initial Military Training, Soldiers will form natural bonds with their fellow Soldiers as part of Army culture. To contribute to this team spirit, we live by the buddy system. A buddy team is usually defined as two Soldiers (same sex) in the same unit who always look after each other.

By getting to know other Soldiers on a professional and personal level, you learn how to improve yourself and encourage others. Working together, you and your battle buddy learn initiative, responsibility, trust, and dependability.

While at the reception battalion, BCT or OSUT, Soldiers are placed in buddy teams. With the requirement to excel in Army training, some Soldiers need more positive reinforcement than others. For that reason, you may also be paired based on your strengths, so you and your buddy can complement each other's weaknesses.

Battle buddy responsibilities:

- Never leave your buddy alone.
- Never let your buddy go into an office or room by themselves; even if a drill sergeant, or instructor says it's okay (If it happens, report it).
- Keep your buddy safe and free from harm.
- Always know the whereabouts of your buddy.
- Pass information to your buddy.
- Encourage and support your buddy to train harder and do better.
- Help your buddy solve problems.
- Inform Cadre of any changes in your buddy's behavior.

In the end, the most rewarding part of the buddy system is making every Soldier your buddy; any buddy could help you accomplish your mission or save your life.

## Recommended practice scope

Focus the graded exercise on the eight `Battle buddy responsibilities` bullets. Keep the introductory and closing paragraphs available as study context rather than requiring one long verbatim recital.

That is a product-level focus, not an official narrowing of the star. The star is attached to the section heading, so the most literal reading is that the full section requires memorization. For this app, the eight actionable responsibilities make the clearest practice target, while the explanatory paragraphs remain available as study context. I found no official source clearly saying the prose must be tested verbatim or that only the bullets are tested.

## Proposed input and grading plan

- Use one multiline list control with eight answer rows.
- Split on line breaks and strip optional bullet or number prefixes.
- Match the eight distinct normalized responses against the canonical bullets as an unordered set. Award one point per distinct matched responsibility; duplicates do not earn additional credit.
- Ignore case, punctuation, apostrophe style, and whitespace while requiring every word of a bullet in order. Do not accept paraphrases.
- Require the parenthetical reporting instruction in the second bullet; it is operationally meaningful, not decorative.
- Easy mode may display the eight first-letter patterns as a shared hint list. Hard mode presents eight blank rows.
- After submission, show only the missing responsibilities and the closest incomplete row rather than repeating all eight as one correction.
